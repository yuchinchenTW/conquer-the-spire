Twice now, and both times at the same height
============================================

the second one. Laid from each run's own peak, on the trainer's own

The floors, the act bosses and the share of climbs that put two bosses
down do not move at all, through either fall. Half the climbs still reach
the third act's boss; they simply stop beating it.

The high marks: 22.8% at update 380160 and 22.5% at 387360. Two runs from
different weights arriving within three tenths of a point of each other
and then falling is not a coincidence.

Played on the same seeds, thirty act 3 boss fights each:

                            well 387360      stopped 397870
    lost                       9 of 30          20 of 30
    the value head there       mean +6.4        mean -6.3
    cards played a turn        5.32             4.58
    potions a fight            2.03             1.71

The same direction as the first collapse (which reached mean -20.3 and
30 of 30), caught earlier.

What this points at, and has not been tested yet: the deep shelf. Two
climbs in five start from a snapshot of an act 2 or act 3 floor, the
shelf holds four thousand of them and is full, and the better the policy
gets the deeper those snapshots sit - so the further in it gets, the more
of its training is the part of the spire it is worst at, and the more the
value head hears that the third act's boss is where climbs end. That
would explain a ceiling that arrives at the same height twice.

The test is one run: carry on from well.pt with --deep 0 and nothing else
changed, and see whether the wins go past 22.5% or fall again at +10000.
