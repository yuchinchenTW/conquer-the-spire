A shelf of last-boss rooms, and what it does to a batch
=======================================================

The fight every run loses is 4.7% of what the trainer learns from. This
adds a second shelf beside the shelf of floors, holding the room in front
of the last boss the run will be asked to beat - whichever act the limit
ends on, not always the third - and takes a share of the deep starts from
it. --deep-boss is that share; --deep is unchanged and still says how
many climbs are started deep at all.

Two knobs, one treatment. A boss climb is short, fifty steps against five
hundred for a whole one, so the share of climbs started there is much
larger than the share of the batch it becomes. Measured on the same
policy, six batches' worth of steps each, rather than assumed:

    setting                act1 fight  act2 fight  act3 fight  act3 boss
    shelf 0.4, boss 0        22.87%      21.51%      19.89%      4.73%
    shelf 0.4, boss 1.0         -           -           -        7.78%
    shelf 0.6, boss 0.8      21.54%      20.93%      16.81%     12.00%
    shelf 0.7, boss 1.0      20.75%      20.44%      13.95%     18.26%

An earlier guess that 40% of climbs would make a fifth of the batch was
out by a factor of four, for exactly that reason. To land in the 15-20%
the experiment is aimed at, the deep share has to go to 0.7 as well.

What it costs: the first two acts lose about two points each, and what
is really displaced is the ordinary act 3 fight, 19.89% to 13.95%. So
the reading has to include whole climbs and not only the boss, which is
what the judge is for.

What is still unproven: that a bigger share of the batch keeps the skill.
The number says what the trainer sees, not what it learns. The run under
bossshelf.bat answers that, and three things have to hold together -
past +15000 updates without the guard firing, 60% or better on the forty
saved rooms in Python/cts_boss.py that it never trains on, and the
judge's whole-climb readings not falling while the boss holds.

The default is 0, so a run that says nothing about this behaves exactly
as it did; there is a test for that beside the one for the shelf itself.
