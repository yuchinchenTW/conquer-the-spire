The guard: stopping a run whose wins have fallen away
=====================================================

Two runs have now gone bad and gone on training for a day: the main run
on 2026-09-17 (act 3 boss fights lost 30 in 30, everything else unmoved -
Notes/act3-boss-collapse-2026-09-17.txt) and, it turns out, the run in
runs\ironclad\before-the-rewind, which fell from 12% wins to 2% at update
80000 and never came back. Both were noticed by a person, hours later.

So the trainer now watches the share of climbs that got as far as they
were asked to get, over the last 60 reports - three hundred updates, about
twenty minutes. Whenever that mean is the highest it has been, the weights
go to well.pt. When it falls under --guard (0.5) of that high mark, the
run stops and says where to pick up from. --guard-least (0.08) is how high
the mark has to be before any of this means anything.

Why the wins and not the return: in the collapse the return only fell a
fifth, because the floors were holding it up. The wins fell by a hundred
and ninety fold.

Replayed against every curve there is, at the settings as shipped:

    run                        curve            what the guard does
    the collapse (0.95)        225545..409827   stops at 401060; best
                                                22.8% at 380160; 8767
                                                updates not wasted
    lambda 0.98, which slid    383185..404778   never fires; best 19.6%
    before-the-rewind          5..113494        stops at 80240; best
                                                12.3% at 65640; 33254
                                                updates not wasted
    before-the-rewind-2,-3,-4  same early run   the same 80240

The 0.98 run is the one that must not fire, and does not: it lost three
and a half points of wins over 21000 updates, which is a worse setting
rather than a run falling over, and stopping it would have thrown away
the comparison it was there to make.

Two thresholds were tried against the same curves. At --guard-least 0.05
the early runs stopped at update 22515, where the wins wobbled between
two and five in a hundred on their way up - a false alarm. At 0.08 that
wobble is beneath the guard and the real fall at 80240 is not. At --guard
0.7 the collapse is caught 5000 updates sooner but the margin over an
ordinary bad patch is thin; at 0.3 it waits 300 updates longer for no gain.

The high mark and its window ride in the checkpoint, so stopping and
starting again does not hand the run a fresh clean slate to fall from.
