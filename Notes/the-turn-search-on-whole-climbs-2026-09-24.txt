Searching the turn, over whole climbs
=====================================

Two hundred climbs a reading, every one played to its end, on two seed
sets: 300005.. which the judge reports on, and 500005.. which nothing has
ever been measured on. The weights are well.pt, update 387360 - the best
any of the five training runs produced - except the last row.

    how it played                   seeds 300005    seeds 500005
                                    won   floors    won   floors
    the policy names its move      29.0%   35.78   25.5%   35.92
    looks at 2, everywhere         36.0%   36.91   39.0%   38.54
    looks at 2 out, turn searched  71.5%   44.24   72.0%   44.11
    the same, best-look2 (356200)                  74.5%   45.06

Two hundred climbs carry a standard error of about three points, so 71.5
and 72.0 are the same number twice, and both are twice what the same
weights manage playing their own moves.

Two bosses down went from 53-54% to 85-87%, and the floors from 36 to 44.
A climb that wins is 48 floors, so an average of 44 is most of them
winning - the three readings agree with each other, which a measurement
artefact would not have to.

The last row matters as much as the rest: best-look2.pt is thirty
thousand updates older and beat the newer weights with the search in
front of it. So what the search is worth does not belong to one
checkpoint, and the training plateau at 22.5% was never the ceiling on
what these weights could play like - only on what they could name.

Nothing about how a win is counted can be reached from here: the engine
writes SPIRE_DONE only when the act 3 boss has actually gone down and the
act limit has been reached (SpireEnv.cpp, the m_bossFight branch). The
search picks moves; it cannot touch that.

What it costs
-------------

About 30ms a decision in a fight, against nothing at all for naming a
move, so a climb takes nine seconds instead of a tenth of one. That is
why the judge still reads look2 - 800 climbs an hour at this price would
be 800 climbs a day - and why --turn is a flag rather than the default.

What is not shown by this
-------------------------

  * The engine is this repository's, not the game's. Every number here is
    against our own rules.
  * Act limit 3, so the spire's own top - act 4 and the Heart - is not in
    any of it.
  * The search is only inside fights. Out of them the one-move looking is
    still what plays, unchanged.
