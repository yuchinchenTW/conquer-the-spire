Every climber kept along the way, on one set of seeds
=====================================================

The judged history only goes back to update 250590, and the readings
before that were taken on other seeds, so none of them could be set
against each other. This puts every checkpoint still on disk through the
same 400 climbs - the judge's reporting seeds, 300005..300404, each one
played to its end - with the policy naming its own move and no looking.

Flat and not the looking, because that is the policy itself; and a win is
a win whatever a point of health was worth in training, so the two older
weights from the days of 0.05 are comparable here where their returns
are not.

So over the fortnight the policy went from 14.5% to 20-25% and the floors
from 33.1 to 35.3, on the same climbs. The rise is real and it is slow.

A caution the table itself supplies: 386960 and 387360 are four hundred
updates apart and read 19.8% and 25.2%. Four hundred climbs carry a
standard error of 2.2 points, and the weights move as well, so a single
checkpoint's reading is worth about five points either way. Only the
blocks in judged.csv - twenty checkpoints at a time - are worth reading
as a level:
