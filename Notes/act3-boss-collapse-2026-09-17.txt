The act 3 boss fight collapsing, and everything else staying
============================================================

The main run (runs\ironclad, lambda 0.95, carrying on from the aim
plateau at 383180) went from 19% flat wins to 0.1% between updates 399000
and 407000 on 2026-09-17, in two steps rather than a slide:

    update band   loss   return   win%   act boss%   two down%   floors
    394000-398800  6.35   41.90   19.5      93.0        50.9      34.3
    399600-400400  5.90   40.10   15.7      93.1        50.1      34.2
    401200-404400  4.42   35.88    5.8      92.9        49.5      34.2
    405200-409200  2.98   33.42    0.6      93.1        48.3      33.7

Every other reading held: floors, the share of climbs that put an act
boss down, the share that put two down, fights won, cards taken, removed,
upgraded, rests. Only the last fight of the spire was being lost.

Played on the same 64 seeds, 30 act 3 boss fights each:

                                383180 (plateau)   409827 (collapsed)
    act 3 boss fights won            16 of 30           0 of 30
    the value head in them       mean +4.3           mean -20.3
                                 (-24.0 .. +21.5)    (-23.6 .. -16.3)
    cards played a turn              5.21               4.22
    potions a fight                  1.61               0.70
    Strike, of the cards played      10.8%              19.0%

The value head had stopped reading the fight at all: within three points
of -20 whatever the board, which is "this climb is over". A zero-cost
Anger sat in hand at the end of a turn. The moves the collapsed weights
name differ from the plateau's on 30-38% of ordinary fight and reward
states and on 63% of act 3 boss states, so the damage is aimed.

Ruled out: the engine (the same dll since 09-07, and the judge hashes it),
the machine (no display or power events in the system log), the settings
(runs\last.bat unchanged, the run never restarted), and chance in the
judge (both judged splits agree, and the trainer's own curve agrees).

Not explained: what tipped it. The two steps look like something
discrete rather than a drift. The entropy pressures were where they had
been all through the aim's rise (0.020/0.010/0.055 against
0.016/0.010/0.063), the rate was on its floor either way, and the same
weights under lambda 0.98 ran 21000 updates without collapsing - while
leaning the same way, 21 of 30 boss fights lost and the value head at
-4.3 in them.

What is kept: aim-plateau.pt (383180), the judged champions
(best-look2.pt of 356200, best-flat.pt of 350610), and
collapsed-409827.pt, which reproduces it.
